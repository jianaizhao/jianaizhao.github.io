# About Me

<div class="info-card" markdown="1">

I am Jianai Zhao, and my citizenship is the People’s Republic of China. I received my BS from the [University of Colorado Boulder](https://www.colorado.edu/), [Ann and H.J. Smead Department of Aerospace Engineering Sciences](https://www.colorado.edu/aerospace/) (United States of America, AeroEngr’22), and my MS from the [Technical University of Darmstadt](https://www.tu-darmstadt.de/index.en.jsp), Mechanical Engineering Department (Federal Republic of Germany, AeroEngr’24). Both degrees are in Aerospace Engineering (Sciences). Before college, I attended Beijing Huijia Private School (Class of 14IB, IBDP’17) and was selected for the Research Science Initiative (RSI-Tsinghua’15) at Tsinghua University, School of Aerospace Engineering.
</div>
